## Design Context

### Users
Travelers considering NEO's four Ohio hotels, guests planning nearby attractions/restaurants/events, and organizers requesting group blocks. The user explicitly requested these booking, local discovery and group inquiry paths.

### Brand Personality
Warm, rooted, confident. The user supplied NEO brand guidelines and requested the line about serving Akron–Canton for over 30 years. Use the supplied Jost/DM Sans typography and Lakeshore Ink, Lamplight, Stone and Harbor Slate palette.

### Aesthetic Direction
User's initial reference was Aimbridge Hospitality. Latest direction: make the site striking; the prior version felt boring. Design decision: cinematic hospitality editorial with real property photos, oversized typography, asymmetric compositions, generous whitespace and controlled movement. No fabricated hotel imagery or testimonials.

### Design Principles
- Let actual hotels lead visually, with direct booking and nearby guide links.
- Use scale and composition for drama, preserving familiar navigation and readability.
- Keep the warm NEO palette and supplied fonts, which take precedence over generic skill font bans.
- Provide a working property slideshow and reduce motion when requested by the device.
- Show only verified events, never fabricated dates or availability.
